PromptShield

Pre-send review for AI support replies

Your support agent drafted a reply to a customer. Before anyone sends it, PromptShield screens the ticket for hidden instructions, masks private data, inspects any script the conversation carries, and checks every claim in the draft against your policy. It gives one recommendation with the evidence. A human always makes the final call - PromptShield sends nothing.
